What the System Can Do
Product & Inventory Control: Effortlessly keep track of what's on your shelves, upload product photos with clean previews, and manage your entire inventory with full create, read, update, and delete capabilities.

Customer Directory: A clean space to save customer profiles, keep tabs on their contact details, and update or remove records whenever needed.

Staff & User Management: Handle team accounts securely using password hashing, profile avatars, and full administrative control over who has access.

Secure Access & Authentication: Protect sensitive management pages behind login checkpoints so only authorized staff can access the backend dashboard.

Smooth Sales Workflow: A dedicated page where staff can pick items, link optional customers, and type in quantities. The system automatically subtracts from your inventory in real-time and prevents overselling if stock runs low.

Sales History & Tracking: Easily review past transactions to see what sold, who bought it, which staff member made the sale, the total price, and the exact timestamp.
